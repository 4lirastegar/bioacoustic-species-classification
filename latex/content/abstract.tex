
\begin{abstract}
This project investigates acoustic insect species classification on InsectSet66, a benchmark of 66 Orthoptera and Cicadidae species.
We extract Mel-frequency cepstral coefficients (MFCC) and log-mel spectrograms from 5-second audio segments and evaluate two complementary approaches.
First, we replicate the mel-spectrogram CNN baseline of Faiss et al.~\cite{faiss2023adaptive}, achieving 60.4\% test accuracy.
Second, we apply k-means clustering, Support Vector Machines (SVM), and Random Forest (RF) on MFCC statistical features.
SVM achieves 76.7\% test accuracy and 0.71 macro-F1, outperforming the unaugmented CNN.
RF feature importance provides interpretable insight into which cepstral coefficients drive species separation.
\end{abstract}
